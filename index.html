📖 QPS Literacy Hub — Practice Checks

A Check is the 10-question quiz at the end of a stop or level. Two Checks at 80% or better = a stop or level passed: the student moves on by themselves. Finished — test them lists what each student passed that you still need to test: ✓ Tested clears it, ↩ Back sends them back to it.

🧪 The Lab — Adding and Subtracting · Multiplying · Place Value · Rounding · 🔎 Reading for Meaning

Students on a plan (a row in the lab-seed tab) come first. Facts lessons (adding, subtracting, multiplying) pass at 90%; place value, rounding and 🔎 Reading for Meaning at 75% (8 of 10 on a lesson, 15 of 20 on the Place Value and Rounding Checks; reading: 6 of 8 points, a Part A + Part B question is worth 2). 📖 QPS = the student's QPS stop now, the same pill as the QPS page (two Checks at 80% move them on), and 🔔 a stop waiting for your test. In reading, 🔁 = the same KIND of mistake again, on a new story (for example "true but not proof"); "Most common" = the kind a student has made most. "Skipped" = all 5 Show-what-you-know questions right. ⭐ = the student's own missed facts added to a lesson; 🔁 = the same wrong answer as an earlier miss. "Still on the list" = facts not yet answered right twice in the Lab.
